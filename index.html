MAEVE Market

A small makers market in someone’s backyard, one Saturday in April. Moms find out what their fellow members make, families eat and catch up, and the kids run around on the grass.

Think neighborhood spring festival, not craft fair.

Saturday in April

  1. 10:00 AM Vendor setup begins
  2. 12:30 PM All vendors set up
  3. 1:00–4:00 Market is open

Exact date & rain date to be set

shop→ eat→ play→ socialize→ discover what your fellow moms make

Who comes

The market is closed to the general public. MAEVE members can attend and bring their family and friends.

We’ll keep the guest count to whatever fits the backyard comfortably, so it stays an easy afternoon rather than a crowd.

The makers

Vendors are MAEVE members who create, design, or offer something of their own. MAEVE pre-approves every vendor and assigns each a spot in the yard.

What makers can sell

  • Handmade physical products
  • Original designs
  • Digital products
  • Services
  • Homemade foods that are legally permitted for sale under Virginia requirements

What vendors handle

  • Their own tables, chairs, and displays
  • Their own payment methods, sales, and money
  • Making sure what they sell follows the law
  • Keeping their table staffed for the whole market

Not included: MLM / direct-sales businesses and resellers. This is for things members make themselves.

More than shopping

Free & community tables
Space for things that aren’t for sale — a book swap, a craft, whatever someone wants to host. These tables are looser and don’t follow the vendor rules.
Potluck
The market doubles as a community potluck. Everyone is welcome to bring a dish; nobody is required to.
Kids
Kids are free to run and play in the yard, and there’ll be a dedicated kids’ area with its own activities — so it feels like a family gathering, not an errand.

Raffle & vendor passport

Several raffle prizes get drawn through the afternoon. Vendors can donate an item to the raffle if they’d like.

Show up 1 ticket
Bring something for the potluck +1
Join a free / community table activity +1
Complete the MAEVE Market Vendor Passport +1

The passport is a small card you fill in as you visit the makers. It gives everyone a reason to walk the whole yard and find people they might have passed by.

The program

About a week before the market, attendees get a simple vendor guide so they know what to look forward to:

  • Vendor list
  • What each vendor makes
  • Map of vendor spots
  • Free & community tables
  • Kids’ activities
  • Everything else worth knowing

For the committee to decide

There’s no traditional vendor fee. The open question is whether vendors chip in, and how food is covered. Three ways it could go:

  1. A

    No fee

    Vendors take part for free and bring something for the potluck.

  2. B

    Small contribution

    Vendors pay a modest amount that goes toward basic food for everyone.

  3. C

    Something else

    Whatever arrangement makes the most sense once we know the event’s needs.

Also still open: the exact Saturday in April and the rain date.

Small, relaxed, family-friendly.
